The accuracy trap

A model that always answers “not fraud” scores this on our data and catches nothing. That’s why we report precision, recall, F1, and PR-AUC.

Honest limits.

  • Simulated dataResults may not transfer to real-world spending.
  • Distance carries no signalIn this generated data, fraud is no farther from home than normal purchases.
  • Fraud drifts over timeThe fraud rate in the test month is lower than in training.
  • Demo is not an evaluationScenario scores illustrate behavior; the results above are the evaluation.
